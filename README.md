# Habit Tracker
A calendar that can track your habits, you can choose and write your habit in the "My New Habit" section, then click on the days where you have achieved your habit to track them. To reset just click on the reset button.
# contents
Contains a calendar, a section where you can chose your habit,and a reset button. If you have completed all your habits a notification saying "Great progress!" shows up.
